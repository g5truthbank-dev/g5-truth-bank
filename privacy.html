Privacy Policy

G5 DR Truth Vault is a private, single-user disaster-recovery application.

Google Drive access is used solely to create, verify, retrieve, and delete encrypted disaster-recovery vault files belonging to the application's owner.

Google account data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for profiling.

Backup payloads are encrypted before being stored in Google Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.
